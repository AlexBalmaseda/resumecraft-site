About

Why we're rebuilding it.

ResumeCraft_AI started as a web platform. It grew broad, tried to serve organisations and individuals at once, and never reached the standard we wanted. So we stopped and started again.

What changed

The new version is for individuals, not organisations, and it is being built as an app rather than a website. One person, one career record, one job to do well.

The method behind it

The idea isn't theoretical. It comes from keeping a career record this way in practice: one master document holding everything, with each claim carrying its date and its evidence, and every application derived from it. That discipline is what the product is meant to give other people.

Where it stands

In development. No release date, no beta list, no pricing. When there is something to try, this page will say so, with a date.

Who builds it

BTC Media Labs LLC, a New York studio. Product design, architecture and decisions are owned by our founder; code implementation is AI-generated under his direction and reviewed by him. See our approach.
