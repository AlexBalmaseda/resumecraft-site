Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website

These are static pages. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no sign-up. We collect nothing from your visit.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub processes the technical information your browser sends in order to deliver the page, including your IP address, under its own privacy practices. We do not receive it.

If you ask to be notified

Using the "notify me" button sends us an email with your address in it. We keep that address for one purpose: to tell you once, when ResumeCraft_AI is available. We don't sell it, share it, or add it to any other list, and there is no other mail to unsubscribe from.

Ask us to remove you at any time, at contact@resumecraftai.io, and we delete it. If we ever stop the project, we delete the list.

The product

ResumeCraft_AI is in development and is not available, so it holds no data about anyone. When there is a product, it will have its own privacy policy, published before anyone can use it.
