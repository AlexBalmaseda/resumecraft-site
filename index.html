One living record of your career.

Most people keep their working life in a dozen half-finished documents, then rewrite the same history for every application. ResumeCraft_AI is being built around the opposite idea: one living record of what you have actually done, from which every application is tailored.

We are rebuilding it from the ground up as an app for individuals. There is no release date yet — but you can ask us to tell you when there is.

The idea

Your career, kept properly once.

Build the record from the documents you already have. Fill the gaps in a guided conversation. Keep every claim tied to when it happened and how you know it. Then let each application draw from that record, instead of starting from a blank page and a vague memory.

What it will do

From a folder of documents to one record you can defend.

This is what we are building. None of it is available yet.

1 · Bring in what you already have

Old résumés, job descriptions, reviews and notes are read and turned into a structured record of roles, dates, work and results — instead of a folder you dread opening.

2 · Fill the gaps in conversation

A guided interview asks about the parts your documents never captured: what the problem was, what you actually did, and how it turned out.

3 · Keep every claim anchored

Each line carries its date and what supports it, so you can stand behind it in an interview rather than hoping nobody asks.

4 · Tailor each application from it

A résumé and covering letter for the specific job, drawn from your record. You review and decide what is true before anything is sent.

Principles

What we're committing to.

These are the rules we are building to, not features you can use today.

Your record is yours

You can read it and take it with you, in a format that is useful elsewhere.

Never invented for you

The point is a record you can defend. Software that embellishes your history would defeat it.

Your career data is not for sale

No selling, no renting, no advertising against your work history.

Nothing is sent without you

Nothing reaches an employer unless you send it.
