In development

One true record of your career.

Most people keep their working life in a dozen half-finished documents, then rewrite the same history for every application. ResumeCraft AI is being built around the opposite idea: one living record of what you have actually done, from which every application is tailored.

We are rebuilding it from the ground up as an app for individuals. There is no release date yet, and nothing to sign up for.

The idea

Your career, kept properly once.

Build the record from the documents you already have. Fill the gaps in a guided conversation. Keep every claim tied to when it happened and how you know it. Then let each application draw from that record, instead of starting from a blank page and a vague memory.

Built from what you have

Old résumés, reviews and notes become a structured record rather than a folder of files.

Every claim keeps its evidence

What you did, when, and what supports it — so you can stand behind it in an interview.

Tailored, not invented

Applications are drawn from your own record. The software drafts; you decide what is true.

Principles

What we're committing to.

These are the rules we are building to, not features you can use today.

Your record is yours

You can read it and take it with you, in a format that is useful elsewhere.

Never invented for you

The point is a record you can defend. Software that embellishes your history would defeat it.

Your career data is not for sale

No selling, no renting, no advertising against your work history.

Nothing is sent without you

Nothing reaches an employer unless you send it.
